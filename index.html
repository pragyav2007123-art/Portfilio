Most-needed items

Quick donation checklist

  • Hygiene: toothpaste, toothbrushes, deodorant, shampoo, soap
  • Food: peanut butter, pasta, rice, canned soups/veg, cereal
  • Baby: diapers, wipes (if accepted by your partner org)

Please donate new/unopened items. Avoid expired goods.

Volunteer teams

What volunteers do

  • Collectors: keep bins organized, record quick counts, and share weekly updates.
  • Packers: sort items into categories, check expiration dates, and build family-ready bundles.
  • Outreach: recruit sponsors, share flyers, and help schools compete in friendly challenges.

FAQ

Quick answers for donors, families, and volunteers.

Donations

What items are accepted?

New/unopened food + hygiene items. Please avoid expired items. If you’re unsure, email us from the Donate section and we’ll confirm.

Can I donate money instead?

Yes — add your official donation link in the Donate section (or tell me what you’re using and I’ll wire it).

Volunteers

Do I need service hours?

We can verify hours when you sign in at volunteer shifts. Ask a coordinator for details.

How do I sign up?

Use the Volunteer form — it creates a pre-filled email draft so we can schedule you quickly.
